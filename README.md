# Financial Aid Documents

Public federal student aid sources, collected as the document corpus for an AI financial aid policy assistant.

## Why this exists

A policy assistant for financial aid is only as good as its sources. Answers must come from official federal guidance, cite the exact document, and change when the guidance changes. This repo holds the source documents so the assistant, its test questions and its results can all point to the same versioned files.

## What is here

| Document | Publisher | Version |
|---|---|---|
| Federal Student Aid Handbook, volumes 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 | U.S. Department of Education, Federal Student Aid | 2024-25 |
| COD Technical Reference, volumes 2 and 6 | U.S. Department of Education, Federal Student Aid | 2025-26 |
| COD Common Record schema change log | U.S. Department of Education, Federal Student Aid | 2025-26 |
| Federal Pell Grant, Direct Loan and Campus-Based program guides | U.S. Department of Education | as published |
| H.R. 1 (One Big Beautiful Bill Act), engrossed amendment text | U.S. Congress | 119th Congress |
| Federal Student Aid summary of OBBBA changes | U.S. Department of Education | as published |

All documents are public federal publications.

## Known gaps

- The Handbook volumes are the 2024-25 edition. Newer editions and Dear Colleague Letters need to be added, with superseded versions kept and labeled, so answers never mix award years.
